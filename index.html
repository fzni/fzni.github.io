01 The Flow

Air is not empty.

Everything moving behind this text is a particle being shoved out of the way by a wireframe chassis. It is a crude, real-time approximation of a wind-tunnel run — no CFD solver, no mesh, just velocity and stubborn air.

Move your cursor through the field. It parts. That is the whole idea.

The same physics decides who wins on Sunday. Ground effect, wake, drag — a Formula 1 car is a wing that happens to have a driver bolted to it. Understanding that is the job I'm training for.
